This page explains how Link Builder works, what information it handles, and your responsibilities when using it.

Link building runs in your browser. You can enter fields manually or use the optional asset picker to fill them from your Bynder portal.

The picker uses a temporary connection. Imported metadata remains in the form after that connection ends, and sharing a generated link also shares the metadata written into its URL.

2. What Link Builder does

Link Builder creates search links for Bynder Digital Asset Management portals using metaproperties, option values, tags, keywords, and asset status filters. It constructs those links in your browser and has no application backend for saving your entered fields or imported metadata.

Using the asset picker is optional. You can build links manually without connecting a Bynder account. Creating a search link does not change assets, publish them, or change their access permissions. The destination portal controls what a recipient can access when they open a link.

3. How the asset picker works

The Browse Assets tab uses Bynder's Universal Compact View to help you select an asset and reuse its metadata. The following describes this site's integration.

Connecting and selecting an asset

  1. Enter your portal domain. Open the asset browser and check the domain shown on the Connect button. Only connect to a portal you recognise and are authorised to use. Opening the picker alone does not sign you in.
  2. Sign in through your portal. Clicking Connect opens a separate sign-in window. Enter your credentials only on your trusted portal or its identity provider. An existing single sign-on session may let you continue without entering them again. Link Builder does not provide a password-entry form.
  3. Browse with a temporary authorisation. After sign-in, this integration receives an access token: a temporary credential it uses to request asset information on your behalf. It requests read access to assets, collections, and metadata. It does not request write permissions or offline access. This authorisation supports browsing and is broader than access to the one asset you eventually select; your portal's permissions still apply.
  4. Select one asset. Its metaproperty names, option names, and tags replace the existing entries in the Metaproperties & Tags tab. Its name is used to identify the import. You can review and edit the fields before copying or opening the generated link. The import does not modify the source asset.

The picker may fetch previews, file details, and other information needed to display assets. The link-builder form receives only the selected asset's name, metaproperty names and options, and tags. Selecting an asset does not upload its original file to a Link Builder server.

Ending the connection

The access token is held in browser memory for the active picker session. This integration does not save access or refresh tokens in persistent browser storage, request background access after the session, or automatically refresh the connection.

Selecting an asset, choosing Close & Disconnect, changing the portal domain, leaving the page, or signing out through the picker ends the local connection. The picker also times out after 15 minutes, or earlier if its access token expires. Reopen it to connect again.

Disconnecting does not clear metadata already imported into the form. It also does not sign you out of your Bynder portal or identity provider, or revoke an authorisation already issued by Bynder. Manage portal sessions and connected-app authorisations through your portal or its administrator.

Availability and permitted use

This connection flow supports Bynder portals, including compatible custom domains; it does not support WebDAM. Access depends on your account permissions, portal configuration, browser popup settings, and the availability of Bynder's services. If connecting or importing fails, you can still enter link fields manually.

Being able to view an asset in your portal does not necessarily mean you are permitted to import its metadata into another tool or share it. Follow your organisation's policies and the asset owner's restrictions.

For background on the component, see Bynder's Universal Compact View documentation.

4. Data handling & privacy

Information in your browser

Your light or dark appearance preference is saved in this browser so it can be used across visits. If you have not chosen a theme, the site follows your system appearance. This preference contains no portal details or account information.

Portal domains, entered search fields, and imported metadata are processed in the open page. Imported fields are not intentionally saved in persistent browser storage. The picker may temporarily remember the connected portal domain locally; that preference is cleared when the picker closes. Local credential entries left by earlier versions of this integration are removed when this site loads.

Your browser may retain page state, cached resources, clipboard contents, or browsing history. Closing the picker does not erase those browser records or copies of links you have already shared. On a shared device, also consider your portal's sign-out options and the browser's privacy controls.

Connections to other services

The picker contacts the selected portal for authentication and asset data, uses Bynder's authentication callback service, and downloads the picker software and display resources from Bynder's distribution services. Asset previews may load from the portal's content hosts. Your portal may also use an external identity provider for sign-in.

These services receive the network requests needed to perform those actions, which can include an IP address and ordinary browser/request information. Their own privacy policies, logging practices, and retention settings apply. The Link Builder does not control their records. Website hosting providers may also maintain ordinary access logs when serving this site.

Analytics and security measures

This site does not load Google Analytics or other usage-tracking scripts. The picker is checked against an approved software fingerprint before it runs, and its API connections are restricted to the selected portal. Its telemetry connections are blocked. These measures reduce exposure; they are not a promise that browser processing is risk-free or that no external service receives information.

5. Your responsibilities & sharing links

  • Use only information you are authorised to handle. Do not use the tool to bypass access controls or to expose confidential information contrary to your organisation's policies.
  • Review the result. Check the portal domain, metadata values, and generated link before using or sharing it. Renamed fields or differences in portal configuration can affect search results.
  • Treat the URL as information you are sharing. Metaproperty names, options, tags, and keywords can appear directly in the generated URL. Copying or sharing a link exposes those values to its recipients, even if they cannot access the underlying assets.
  • Consider where links travel. Opening a link sends its search parameters to the destination portal. Links may also be retained in browser history, destination logs, messages, or other services where you paste them. Do not put passwords, access tokens, or other secrets into search fields.
